Licence calculator

What a licence costs over time

₹50 lakh
5 years

Royalty runs for 10 years from first sale, paid quarterly. Exclusive licences carry a minimum annual royalty of ₹50,000 from Year 3. Option D also gives CARR 3% equity in your company.

Benchmarks: robotics equipment at NIT Calicut (Kinova Gen3, ₹1,000/hr); startup tier at about half the industry rate at the IIT (BHU) Central Instrument Facility; consultancy at ₹15,000–25,000 per man-day at IIT Palakkad; institute equity of 2–9.5% under the national Innovation and Startup Policy.
